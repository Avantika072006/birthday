My Love

The more i understood her , The more beautiful she came out. My piyari butter who talks a lott , but won't utter a word about her feelings. She won't even let you know, will just give hints , it's upto you if you get it or not. She loves using her mind , loves things that makes her mind work, loves deep conversations and is good at questioning. I may have never met her but through screen only she feels my own being . Thinking about her makes me excited. Maybe in the starting i couldn't fully understand her but it's so beautiful to know her like this. I'll always have one regret that I'm not one of her local friends,maybe then i could understand her better ,maybe then we could talk about things face to face , maybe then we could even hug. She expresses herself in levels or all at once . Mostly i feel i disappointed her , once she said she gets bored because I don't play mind games , I guess that's trueee, I'm just a boring friend , I don't bring anything in a friendship. She loves telling things but only if she knows that the other one is willing to listen. One thing is so cuteeee about herrr that she doesn't say I love you like that's so adorable but ofc i know she does. One more thing I admire about her is that she gives you what you give her like a reflection, so it's ur part to work on, and I love working on . I have never expressed myself this simply to anyone before like I'm totally different with her I can't even control being different, the personality i show to her is my real one , others never met that. One fear will always remain that what if I disappoint her . Meri Punjabi kudi bold , expressive at everything but not when it comes to the topic of Aleeza. Kitna kuchh bolti hai but apne liye kuchh bhi nhi bolti. She's expressive with me about all the other thingssssss. Happy soul carrying everything within her , literally everything. She thinks that I don't know herrr that's not totally truee , I know you my butter jan i just don't show the only thing I know is if I were given a chance to be with you in real I'd have known you better and more .
